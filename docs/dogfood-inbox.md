- [2026-09-12] FT4 the Channels selection is different to FT8: 2720Hz, 2804Hz, 452Hz, etc — triaged 2026-09-12: working as designed, not a defect. A Channels cell is exactly one signal wide and the grid starts at the passband floor (`Ft8OccupancyStrip.svelte`: `passbandLow + i × signalWidth`); the width comes from the active profile (`internal/ft8/profile.go`: FT8 50 Hz, FT4 84 Hz = ceil(4 × 20.833 Hz)), so FT4 cells fall on 200 + 84k (452 = k 3, 2720 = k 30, 2804 = k 31) and there are 33 of them instead of 56. Only `docs/ft8.md` still describes the strip in FT8-only terms (~50 Hz cells, ≈56 across 200–3000; lines ~535, ~562, ~999, ~1148) — wording fixed 2026-09-12 in `docs/ft8.md` (strip, Spectrum, detection tiers, structural constants; the dated step (e) record gained a pointer), uncommitted.
