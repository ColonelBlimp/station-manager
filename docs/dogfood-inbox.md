- [2026-09-11] FT4 live run: with a second SPA tab open (Map), the map reported "cannot reach the daemon" and Band Activity looked unable to keep up with 7.5 s slots; the daemon log showed its SSE subscriber count (global across event endpoints) reach 6 and stream connections lasting 11–27 s. The two tabs together held six streams: the shell's log and rig streams in each, the FT8 stream in Operate, and the Map view's own second log stream. Inference from this incident (not a cited limit): six long-lived streams fill a ~6-per-host HTTP/1.1 connection cap in the browser, so ordinary requests (map fetch, enrichment, contest-dupe, count) queue behind them; closing the extra tabs restored the map at once (measured). Follow-ups: (a) the map should reuse the shell's always-on log stream (ADR 0079) instead of opening its own; (b) cap enrichment lookup concurrency in the SPA (23 first-sight lookups in one minute at 1.5–2.5 s each) so operator actions never queue behind them; (c) longer term HTTP/2 over TLS, which would lift such a cap (inference, to verify); (d) document "one tab" until then.
