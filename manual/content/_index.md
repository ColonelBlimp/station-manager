---
title: Station Manager — Operator Manual
---

Station Manager (SM) is a ham radio logger. You start it once in the background
and operate it from your web browser — logging contacts, controlling your rig,
running FT8, and forwarding your log to online services.

Use the contents on the left to jump to a topic, or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>F</kbd>
(<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>F</kbd> on a Mac) to search this whole page.
