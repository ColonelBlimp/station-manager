*Draft outline — content to be written.*

- What SM does: a local logbook that also forwards your contacts to online services.
- The model in one screen: a background program (the *daemon*) plus a browser window — start it once, log in the browser.
- What you need: a web browser, optionally a supported rig, and accounts for any upload services you use.
- Attended operation only — SM never operates the radio on its own.
