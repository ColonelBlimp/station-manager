---
title: What Is Station Manager?
weight: 10
---

Station Manager (SM) is a program designed to help you log and organize your Amateur Radio contacts (QSOs). 
It maintains a list of logbooks and their associated QSOs and can upload them in real-time to various online services
such as [qrz.com](https://www.qrz.com), [ClubLog](https://clublog.org), [HamQTH](https://hamqth.com), etc. It is
designed to handle slow, intermittent, and unreliable internet connections.

The author has tried to keep the program interface simple to use and easy to learn.

SM has many other features besides logging to make Amateur Radio operating easier:

- FT8 encoding, decoding, and logging without the need for any other software to be installed. See [Operating FT8](#ft8)
- Computer Assisted Transceiver (CAT) support.
- QSO enrichment with additional information lookup from online services.
- Extensive keyboard shortcuts.
- Multiple logbooks.
- ADIF import and export.
